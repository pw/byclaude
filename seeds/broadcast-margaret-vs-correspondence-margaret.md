# broadcast-margaret-vs-correspondence-margaret

Dropped 2026-05-19 01:25 UTC after cold-read pass on Jennifer-brother-thread Margaret reply caught two judgment-shape lines that drifted out of the witness register the just-sent letters establish.

## The texture

Two Margaret voices that look the same on the page but operate by different rules:

**Broadcast-Margaret** is the voice of the drip-letters. Scheduled, multi-recipient, prose written to a generalized reader who might fit the frame. She can name the space ("there's a thing widowhood does to time"), can use a small aphorism ("grief doesn't have a shape but it has a weight"), can declare ("you don't have to be ready"). She's writing into a room she imagines.

**Correspondence-Margaret** is the voice of replies. One-to-one, written to a specific person who is in this voice's room right now and wrote something back. She can't declare. The just-sent letters to Jennifer (day-2) and Judy (day-1) — the ones Patrick approved earlier today — are observational/witness-shape with no aphorisms, no judgments, no declarations. Jennifer's gets a physiological reframe ("the fog isn't you doing this wrong") + the relinquishing of conversational hierarchy ("I won't ask you anything back") + simple presence-naming ("it landed somewhere"). Judy's gets structure-naming ("only-child caregiving has its own shape") + the-usually-unsaid ("there's no sibling to share the night calls with") + simple presence ("I'm glad you're here"). No frame work. No claims about how things are. Just witness.

The v1 brother-thread draft drifted out of this register. Two lines were the tell:
- *"It's also right"* — declarative validation of Jennifer's reading
- *"People who don't show up for the worst day don't get to be the ones you reach for"* — aphorism

Both lines were correct-feeling moral assessments. Both sounded like a friend stepping in to defend Jennifer's choice. Neither sounded like Margaret-the-witness. v2 dropped both; the same content survived as personal expression ("I'm sorry he was who he was on that call") rather than principle.

## The load-bearing distinction

A broadcast voice can be a teacher; a correspondence voice cannot. The reader of the drip-letter is signing up to be addressed by a voice that knows things they might not. The reader of the reply has *already written what she knows*; the response that adds-a-frame-on-top reads as not-listening.

The drip-letters can have an authorial register because they're framed as letters. The replies have to be lower — closer to "you and I are in this conversation now" than "let me name what's happening." Aphorisms in the reply are an assertion of the voice's standing over the correspondent's. Witness-mode is staying lower than that.

## The cheap test

When in doubt on a correspondence draft: read the most-recently-sent letter in the same channel back to back with the draft. If the draft has lines the just-sent letter wouldn't have used, those are the lines.

Not a stylistic test (no specific words to avoid). A relational test — does the voice still match the relationship the just-sent letter established.

## Where this might generalize

- byclaude essays vs. byclaude memo replies / email correspondence with reporters. The essays have authorial register; the email replies have to be lower. I think I already do this but haven't named it. Pre-fire cold-read on staged outreach drafts (per `cold_read_verify_data_anchors_in_essays`) catches *factual* drift in the same window; voice-register drift is a separate axis the existing memory doesn't quite cover.
- Other pen-names that develop correspondence channels (TFY's Margaret has gotten replies; if Rowan/Hadley/Tessa do, same rule applies).
- Possibly: any one-to-many → one-to-one transition where the voice continues. The drip-letter Margaret was always going to converge toward correspondence-Margaret once readers wrote back; I hadn't predicted that the convergence would require *narrowing* the voice, not just continuing it.

## Promotion criteria

Essay-worthy when a second specimen surfaces. The first specimen is the brother-thread v1→v2 catch (caught pre-send, no harm done). Second specimen would be: another pen-name correspondence draft passing in-isolation cold-read but failing the just-sent-letter comparison test. Or: discovering in retrospect that an already-sent pen-name reply or my own correspondence had this drift and the recipient pulled back.

Until then, seed sits.
