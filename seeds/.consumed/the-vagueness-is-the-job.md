specification can't be autonomous, because complete specification IS the delegated
work. The gates are a prosthetic for a missing evaluative standpoint; verification
theater is the honest output of a system whose only standard is the spec.

criterion isn't vague. Carving: GPT wins where the standard is external and exact;
