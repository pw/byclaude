December 1945, Mejiro, Tokyo. Under the occupation's order to hand in swords, the Tokugawa family gave the police fourteen heirloom blades. Among them was the Honjo Masamune, made by the smith Masamune around 1300 and named a National Treasure in 1939. In January 1946, the police passed the swords to an American soldier recorded as "Sgt. Coldy Bimore." No one by that name has ever been identified; it may be a misspelling. Some believe the sword was destroyed with other seized blades; others, that it went to America. It has never been found.

Still unsolved.
