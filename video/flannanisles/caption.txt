December 1900, Eilean Mòr in the Outer Hebrides. A passing ship saw that the Flannan Isles lighthouse was dark. Bad weather held off the relief boat until Boxing Day. When it landed, the three keepers, James Ducat, Thomas Marshall and Donald McArthur, were gone. The lamps were cleaned and ready, the clock had stopped, and one set of oilskins was still inside. At the west landing, iron railings were twisted and a block of stone weighing over a ton had been moved. The inquiry believed a great wave swept them away. No trace of the three men was ever found.

Still unsolved.
