June 5, 1960, Lake Bodom, near Espoo, Finland. Four teenagers were camping in a tent on the shore. In the early morning, someone attacked the tent from outside. Three of them were killed. One survived. The weapons were never found, and keys to the motorcycles were missing while the motorcycles were left behind. In 2004 police arrested the survivor; in 2005 a court acquitted him. No one has ever been convicted.
