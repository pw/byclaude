March 1918, Barbados. The US Navy coal ship Cyclops left port for Baltimore with roughly 300 people aboard. She was carrying about 10,000 tons of manganese ore for wartime munitions. She never arrived. No distress call was ever received. No wreckage was ever found. A storm, a broken hull, a German submarine — none has ever been proven.

Still unsolved.
