On 16 August 1942, the US Navy blimp L-8 lifted off from Treasure Island in San Francisco Bay on patrol, with two officers aboard. Near the Farallon Islands, they radioed that they were investigating an oil slick. They never radioed again. Hours later, the airship drifted ashore, touched down on a beach, and came to rest in a street in Daly City. The gondola door was open, and no one was inside. The parachutes, the life raft and a briefcase of classified papers were still aboard, and the radio worked. Neither man was ever found.

Still unsolved.
