Moscow, 1584. Ivan the Terrible died, and legend says his library lay hidden in vaults beneath the Kremlin. The story says its Greek and Latin manuscripts came from Constantinople with Sophia Palaiologina, the Byzantine princess who married his grandfather in 1472. In the 1800s a professor reported a list of its books, including all 142 books of Livy's History of Rome. Only 35 survive today. The original list was never found, and it may be a forgery. In 1933 an archaeologist dug beneath a Kremlin tower and found old passages, but no books. Some historians think the library burned in Moscow's fires; others, that it never existed. No trace of it has ever been found.

Still unsolved.
