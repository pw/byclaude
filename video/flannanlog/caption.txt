December 1900, the Flannan Isles lighthouse in Scotland's Outer Hebrides. The keepers' last log entries are often quoted: a great storm, a keeper weeping, and a final line, "God is over all." None of those words are in the official record. They were printed in a 1965 book, credited to a 1929 magazine story that the historian Mike Dash concluded was fiction. The official report says the log ended on 13 December, and a chalk slate carries the routine to the morning of the 15th: the lamp cleaned, the oil filled. The entries were invented. The disappearance was not. The three keepers were never found.

Still unsolved.
