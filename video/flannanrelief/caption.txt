Boxing Day 1900, the Flannan Isles lighthouse in Scotland's Outer Hebrides. The relief ship fired a rocket, and no one answered. The relief keeper, Joseph Moore, went ashore alone and found the entrance gate and the outer door closed. In a letter two days later he wrote that the fire had not been lit for some days, and the beds were empty, just as the men had left them in the early morning. He did not search further; he knew something serious had happened. That night he and three men from the ship lit the light again. James Ducat, Thomas Marshall and Donald McArthur were never found. Moore's letter went on public display for the first time in 2026.

Still unsolved.
