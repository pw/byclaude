6 July 1907, Dublin Castle. In the library of the Office of Arms, the safe was found closed but unlocked. It had not been forced. The case inside, which held Ireland's Crown Jewels, a diamond star and jewelled badge of the Order of St Patrick, was empty, and five gold collars were gone too. It was four days before a royal visit. The jewels have never been recovered, and no one was ever charged.

Still unsolved.
