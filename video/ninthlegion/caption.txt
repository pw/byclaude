Around AD 108, York, Roman Britain. Rome's Ninth Legion was based at the fortress there. A stone carved for a gate of the fortress is its last securely dated record in Britain. By the 120s, another legion was at York. Tiles stamped with the Ninth's name turned up at Nijmegen, in the Netherlands, but when they were made is disputed. Later lists of Rome's legions do not include it. Lost in Britain, moved to the Rhine, or sent east: all remain theories.

Still unsolved.
