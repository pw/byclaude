1941, Tsarskoye Selo, Russia: the Amber Room, a palace room walled in carved amber, had been a gift from Prussia in 1716. That autumn it was taken down by German forces in 36 hours and sent to Königsberg Castle. It was displayed there, then packed into crates. In early 1945, the trail ends. Some believe it burned; others think it went by ship or into a bunker. None of it has been proven. One mosaic surfaced in Germany in 1997, and the room in the palace today is a copy. The original was never found.

Still unsolved.
