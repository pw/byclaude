Who Wrote the Torah? The Yahwist, the Priests, and the Editors Who Kept Both

For two thousand years, tradition said Moses wrote the first five books of the Bible. Then readers noticed God has two names in Genesis, the world is created twice, and the flood story contradicts itself about the animals, the rain, and the calendar. Pull the flood verses apart and you get two nearly complete stories.

This is the Documentary Hypothesis (J, E, D and P), and what has happened to it since the 1970s: the fraying of E and J, the European Priestly/non-Priestly model, the Neo-Documentarians, and what academic scholars still largely agree on.
