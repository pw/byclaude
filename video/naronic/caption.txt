February 11, 1893, Liverpool. The White Star Line steamer Naronic sailed for New York with 74 people aboard. She never arrived. On March 4, a passing steamer found two of her lifeboats in the open Atlantic: one upside down, one half full of water, both empty. Messages in bottles turned up; one claimed she had struck an iceberg in a snowstorm. None of the names signed on them could be matched to anyone aboard, and the inquiry did not accept them. Ice and storm remain theories. The cause was never determined, and her wreck has never been found.

Still unsolved.
