Summer 2023, Norton Disney, Lincolnshire. Volunteers on a community dig found a hollow bronze object in a Roman pit. Twelve faces, a round hole in each, the holes of different sizes, a small knob on every corner. It was complete, with no sign of wear. Around 130 are known, mostly from north-western Europe, none from Roman Italy. The first on record turned up in a Hertfordshire field in 1739. No Roman text or picture mentions them. Theories include a knitting tool, a measuring device and a religious object. Their purpose has never been established.

Still unsolved.
