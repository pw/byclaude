March 1952, Qumran, near the Dead Sea. In Cave 3, archaeologists found a scroll made of copper. Too brittle to unroll, it was sawn into strips and read. It lists 64 hiding places, and the gold and silver said to lie at nearly all of them. By some estimates, the total runs to tons. In the early 1960s, an expedition dug at some of the sites and found nothing. Temple treasure, legend, or hoax? Scholars still disagree.

Still unsolved.
