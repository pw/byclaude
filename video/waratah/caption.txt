July 1909, off the coast of South Africa. The Waratah, a new liner on only her second voyage, left Durban for Cape Town and never arrived. 211 people were aboard, and none was ever seen again. Early on July 27, another steamer exchanged lamp signals with her, the last confirmed sighting. Decades of searching have found other wrecks. The Waratah has never been found.

Still unsolved.
