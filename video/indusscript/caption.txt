More than 4,000 years ago, in the Indus Valley of present-day Pakistan and northwest India, people carved small stone seals, most with an animal and a short row of signs. More than 4,000 inscribed objects have been found. The average text is just five signs long, drawn from roughly 400 different signs. No bilingual text, no Rosetta Stone, has ever been found. Some scholars have argued the signs may not be writing at all. More than 100 decipherments have been proposed, and in 2025 the Tamil Nadu government offered a $1 million prize. No proposed reading has been accepted.
