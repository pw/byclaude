April 1921, Buxton, near Cape Hatteras, North Carolina. A local fisherman turned in a note he said he had found in a bottle. Ten weeks earlier, the schooner Carroll A. Deering had been found aground on Diamond Shoals, sails set, her crew gone. The note claimed the Deering had been captured by another boat. Five federal departments investigated, and handwriting experts concluded the note was forged. The fisherman later admitted forging it, reportedly hoping for work at the lighthouse. The real crew was never found.

Still unsolved.
