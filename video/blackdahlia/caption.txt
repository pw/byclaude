January 15, 1947, Los Angeles. Elizabeth Short, 22, from Medford, Massachusetts, was found murdered in a vacant lot. Days later, a newspaper received an envelope holding her birth certificate, her photographs and her address book, wiped with gasoline. Then came letters signed "Black Dahlia Avenger." One promised to surrender. No one came. Over the years, hundreds of people confessed. None held up. No one has ever been charged with her murder.
