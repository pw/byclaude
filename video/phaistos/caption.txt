July 1908, the Minoan palace of Phaistos, Crete. Archaeologists found a disc of fired clay, about 16 cm across. Its two faces carry spirals of 242 signs in all, each pressed into the wet clay with a stamp. Forty-five different signs, and no other disc like it has ever been found. No trace of the stamps has been reported. It has been read as a prayer, a hymn, a list. More than a century on, no reading has been accepted.
