September 18, 1679, Lake Michigan. Le Griffon, the first full-sized sailing ship on the upper Great Lakes, sailed from Washington Island for Niagara. Her cargo was furs. Six men were aboard: her pilot and five crew. She never arrived, and no one aboard was ever seen again. A friar from the expedition wrote of a violent storm; La Salle believed his own crew had sunk her and taken the furs. Wrecks in Lake Michigan and Lake Huron have been claimed as hers. None has ever been confirmed.

Still unsolved.
