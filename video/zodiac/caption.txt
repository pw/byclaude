August 1969, Northern California. Three newspapers received letters from a killer, each holding one third of a code. He demanded they print it. Within about a week, a Salinas schoolteacher and his wife had broken it. Months later came a second code: 340 symbols. It stayed unsolved for 51 years, until three amateur codebreakers cracked it in 2020. It did not contain his name. Two shorter codes have never been confirmed solved, and the writer has never been officially identified.
