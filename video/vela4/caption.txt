September 22, 1979, the ocean south of Africa. An American satellite recorded a double flash of light, the signature of a nuclear test. That day, President Jimmy Carter wrote in his diary of an indication of a nuclear explosion: "either South Africa, Israel using a ship at sea, or nothing." Air Force planes flew 25 sorties over the region to sample the air and found no fallout. In February 1980 he wrote of "a growing belief among our scientists" that Israel had conducted a nuclear test there. Publicly, a White House panel called the flash probably not nuclear. The diary was published in 2010, and no government has ever confirmed a test.

Still unsolved.
