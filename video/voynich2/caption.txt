Yale University's Beinecke Library holds manuscript MS 408, better known as the Voynich manuscript. Its calfskin pages were carbon dated in 2009 to between 1404 and 1438. It is written in an alphabet of its own, about twenty-odd characters that no one has matched to any known language. Its drawings show plants nobody can name, circular charts like star maps, and pools of water linked by pipes. Wartime codebreakers, linguists and computers have all tried. No reading has ever been proved.

Still unsolved.
