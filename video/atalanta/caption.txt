January 1880, the Royal Naval Dockyard in Bermuda. The training ship HMS Atalanta sailed for England with about 280 aboard, most of them young trainees. She never arrived. None of them was ever seen again. Wreckage was reported near the Azores, but none of it was ever proven to be hers. A former crewman said she rolled dangerously; no trace of her was ever found. Theories include a storm and an unstable ship. No wreck has ever been identified.

Still unsolved.
