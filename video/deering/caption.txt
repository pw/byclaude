January 31, 1921, Diamond Shoals, off Cape Hatteras, North Carolina. The schooner Carroll A. Deering was found aground with her sails still set. Her lifeboats, log and navigation equipment were gone. Food appeared laid out for a meal, and a cat was aboard. Of the captain and crew, there was no sign. Five federal departments investigated piracy, mutiny, rum-runners and a hurricane; none was proven. The crew was never found.

Still unsolved.
