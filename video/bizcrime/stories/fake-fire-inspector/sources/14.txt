HEADLINE: Ex-Con Arrested After Pretending To Be Fire Inspector: Police
PUBLISHED: 2018-02-02T16:11:00Z
AUTHOR: Daniel Hubbard

Michael Carrion, 52, of Bronx, New York, was charged with theft and wrongful impersonation, said Detective Capt. Laurence Martin.

Police responded to the Cinnabon bakery at the Willowbrook Mall Nov. 8 on a report of a man pretending to be from "city fire prevention" who was there to refill fire extinguishers, Martin said.

Carrion allegedly checked the bakery, but did not refill the extinguishers and filled out a receipt for $476.50, Martin said.

Wayne Police Detective Harry Ellis investigated and learned that similar occurrences were reported in North Jersey and Carrion was identified as the suspect, the captain said.

Carrion would, allegedly, in each case state that he was there from "City Fire Inspection" to conduct a fire inspection and provided people with a hand-written receipt, Martin said. "City Fire Inspection" was even on the receipts Carrion provided.

Carrion was arrested at his Bronx, New York basement apartment Jan. 23. He was wanted by law enforcement agencies in Fort Lee, Carlstadt, Fairfield, Rochelle Park, Pennsylvania, Virginia, California, Texas, Massachusetts and Missouri, Martin said.
