# ww-pounds — sources

Film: the pounds (loading) formula, lb/day = flow (MGD) × concentration (mg/L) × 8.34 lb/gal. Original question —
not taken from any exam, vendor item bank or prep company. Figures are labelled `example` on the question card; the
end card says `Check your state's exam and formula sheet.` The on-screen text names no certifying body.

## Rules and their authority

1. **Loading rate formula (the certifier's formula sheet), unchanged between the 2018 and the 2023 editions.**
   - 2018 edition, "Wastewater Treatment, Collection, Industrial, Laboratory Formula/Conversion Table", Copyright
     © May 31, 2018 by Association of Boards of Certification, page 3 of 8.
     URL: https://www.gowpi.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/ABC-formula-conversion-2018-WW-1.pdf
     > "Loading Rate, lb/day* = (Flow, MGD)(Concentration, mg/L)(8.34 lb/gal)"
   - 2023 edition, "Wastewater Treatment, Collection, Industrial Waste, & Wastewater Laboratory Formula/Conversion
     Table", Copyright © 2023 by Water Professionals International.
     URL: https://nywea.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/WWT-FCT_081023_with-Pie-Wheels.pdf
     > "Loading Rate, lb/day* = (Flow, MGD)(Concentration, mg/L)(8.34 lb/gal)"
     (identical wording to 2018; the same page also has "Mass, lb* = (Volume, MG)(Concentration, mg/L)(8.34 lb/gal)")

2. **Same formula, specifically for BOD, from a state regulator's exam formula sheet.** Indiana Department of
   Environmental Management, "Formula Sheet for all Wastewater Operator Exams", Revised 3/17, formula F017.
   URL: https://www.in.gov/idem/cleanwater/files/wastewater_cert_study_guide_formula_sheet.pdf
   > "BOD loading, lbs/day = (Flow rate, MGD) x (BOD, mg/L) x 8.34"

3. **What 8.34 is: the weight of one US gallon of water — on the same conversion line as 3.785 L.** 2023 table,
   conversion factors:
   > "1 gallon (US) ................. = 3.785 L" / "= 8.34 lb of water"
   (2018 table: "1 gallon (US) ............................. = 3.785 L" on the same entry.) Also California State
   Water Resources Control Board, proposed equivalent formula sheet:
   URL: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/water_issues/programs/operator_certification/docs/proposed_equivalent_formula_sheet.pdf
   > "1 gal (water) = 8.34 lb"

## Worked example (illustrative figures)

Influent flow 3.2 MGD, influent BOD 200 mg/L.

- flow × concentration: 3.2 × 200 = 640
- × 8.34 lb/gal: 640 × 8.34 = 5337.6 → 5,337.6 lb/day  → answer **D) 5,337.6 lb/day** (exact, no rounding)

The distractors, each a real slip with a stated reason:
- A) 76.7 lb/day — divides by 8.34 instead of multiplying: 640 ÷ 8.34 = 76.738… → 76.7.
- B) 640.0 lb/day — forgets the 8.34 entirely (3.2 × 200 = 640), so the result is not in pounds. This is the trap
  line on screen: "B) 640.0 forgets × 8.34, the pounds in a gallon of water. Without it, the answer isn't in pounds."
- C) 2,422.4 lb/day — uses 3.785 (litres per gallon, the neighbouring value on the same conversion line) instead
  of 8.34: 640 × 3.785 = 2422.4.

Other numbers on screen: the formula line "lb/day = MGD × mg/L × 8.34", the note "8.34 lb = 1 gallon of water"
(1 gallon, from source 3), the stack 3.2 / 200 / 640 / 8.34 / 5,337.6 (above); countdown ring digits 5, 4, 3, 2, 1
(a five-second pause timer, not data).
