# ww-svi — sources

Film: sludge volume index (SVI) from a 30-minute settleometer reading and the MLSS. Original question — not taken
from any exam, vendor item bank or prep company. Figures are illustrative (labelled `example` on the question card;
the end card says `Check your state's exam and formula sheet.`).

## Rules and their authority

1. **The formula as it appears on the ABC wastewater formula sheet** (the sheet handed out with ABC exams).
   Association of Boards of Certification, *Wastewater Treatment, Collection, Industrial, Laboratory
   Formula/Conversion Table*, Copyright May 31, 2018, page 4 of 8. Copy fetched from:
   https://www.gowpi.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/ABC-formula-conversion-2018-WW-1.pdf
   > "Sludge Volume Index, mL/g = (SSV30, mL/L)(1,000 mg/g) / MLSS, mg/L"
   Abbreviations, same sheet: "SSV30 .... settled sludge volume 30 minute" · "MLSS .... mixed liquor suspended solids"
   Same page: "Sludge Density Index = 100 / SVI" (a different quantity — not asked here).

2. **Same formula on a state regulator's sheet.** Florida Department of Environmental Protection, *Wastewater
   Treatment Formulas/Conversion Table*, revised February 2014.
   URL: https://floridadep.gov/sites/default/files/ww-abcd-formula_0.pdf
   > "Sludge Volume Index or SVI = 30 min settling, ml/L (1,000) / Mixed Liquor Suspended Solids, mg/L"

3. **What the number means, and the ratio's direction (volume on top, solids on the bottom).** Michigan Department
   of Environment, Great Lakes, and Energy (EGLE), Operator Certification, *Activated Sludge Process Control Manual*,
   p. 21.
   URL: https://www.michigan.gov/-/media/Project/Websites/egle/Documents/Programs/WRD/Operator-Certification/activated-sludge-manual.pdf?rev=18ceb928163f4dac8689e2f34dd365ae
   > "The definition for SVI is: The volume in milliliters occupied by one gram of activated sludge which has
   > settled for 30 minutes."
   > "SVI = mls Settled / MLSS, mg/l / 1000"
   > "The SVI of 108 indicates that each gram of settled sludge will occupy a volume of 108 milliliters."
   (Supports the on-screen line "Each gram of sludge settles into 125 mL." for this example.)

## Worked example (illustrative figures)

Given: settled sludge volume after 30 minutes (SSV30) = 250 mL/L; MLSS = 2,000 mg/L.

    SVI = SSV30 × 1,000 ÷ MLSS
        = 250 × 1,000 ÷ 2,000
    250 × 1,000 = 250,000
    250,000 ÷ 2,000 = 125
    SVI = 125 mL/g

Check by the Michigan form: 2,000 mg/L ÷ 1000 = 2 g/L; 250 ÷ 2 = 125. Same answer.

→ answer **C) 125 mL/g**. Meaning: each gram of sludge settles into 125 mL.

The distractors, each wrong for a stated reason:
- A) 0.125 mL/g — forgets the × 1,000 (mg to g): 250 ÷ 2,000 = 0.125.
- B) 8 mL/g — flips the ratio: 2,000 ÷ 250 = 8. This is the trap line on screen: "B) 8 mL/g flips the ratio:
  settled volume goes on top, MLSS on the bottom."
- D) 8,000 mL/g — flips the ratio and keeps the × 1,000: 2,000 × 1,000 ÷ 250 = 8,000.

Other numbers on screen: "30" (the 30-minute settling time; also inside "SSV30"); countdown ring digits
5, 4, 3, 2, 1 (a five-second pause timer, not data).
