I checked all seven against FIFA's own documents, so none rests on memory. The main source is the *FIFA Football Agent Exam Study Materials – January 2026 edition*, which contains the current FFAR text and Circular 1873. For Q1 I also used the official *FIFA Football Agent Exam Rules* PDF. **Every question has one clearly correct answer, and none is ambiguous.**

**Q1 — A.** The Exam Rules define Study Materials as available "to consult, in digital format only, during the Exam Session." Permitted items include "The Study Materials (accessed only electronically on the approved Devices)." Using any software, websites or materials other than the Platform, the Safe Exam Browser, the Study Materials and the Unofficial Translator Tool gets a candidate disqualified. So B (paper) is wrong, C (any website) is wrong, and D is wrong because the exam is open-book. No other option holds up.

**Q2 — C.** FFAR Art. 12 para 3 in the current edition (para 10 in the 2023 original): an agreement with an Individual "may not exceed two years. This term may be extended by a new Representation Agreement only. Any automatic renewal provision … shall be null and void." A is directly contradicted. D confuses the rule with the separate advice requirement in para 4, which is about telling the client to consider independent legal advice, not about term length. Circular 1873 did not suspend the two-year limit. Clubs (Engaging or Releasing Entities) have no maximum term, but the question is about a player. Not ambiguous.

**Q3 — D.** Art. 12 para 1 says services may only be performed after a written Representation Agreement is signed. Art. 14 para 1 says a fee may be charged "as agreed in a Representation Agreement." Options A, B and C each drop the requirement for a prior written agreement. Not ambiguous.

**Q4 — B.** Art. 11 para 4(a) in the current numbering: "The following natural or legal persons may not have an Interest in any affairs of a Football Agent or their Agency: a) Clients." There is no route through disclosure, FIFA approval or player consent, so A, C and D all fail. Not ambiguous.

**Q5 — B.** Art. 13 para 1: an Approach is allowed "no more than six months before the minor reaches the age where they may sign their first professional contract in accordance with the law applicable…", and only after "prior written consent … from the minor's legal guardian." C wrongly ignores local law, D names the wrong period and the wrong person giving consent, and A leaves out the time limit. Not ambiguous.

**Q6 — D.** Art. 17 para 1(b): if the annual licence fee isn't paid by the Platform deadline, the licence "shall automatically be provisionally suspended." Para 4(b): if the agent doesn't fix it "within 60 days … their licence shall be withdrawn." The Disciplinary Committee only handles eligibility cases (para 3), not unpaid fees. Not ambiguous.

**Q7 — C.** Circular 1873 lists the suspended provisions (following the Dortmund injunction):
- the service fee cap (Art. 15 paras 1–4)
- the client-pays rule
- the timing and payment of fees
- double representation (Art. 12 paras 8–10)
- some reporting obligations
- disclosure and publication (Art. 19)
- the submission rule
- the Clearing House payment requirement

The two-year term, the exam requirement and CPD are not on the list. Not ambiguous.

One thing for the kit brief: the current FFAR numbering differs from the 2023 original (two-year term is now Art. 12.3, not 12.10; client interest is Art. 11.4). Circular 1873 still uses the 2023 numbering, so its "Art. 12 paras 8–10" doesn't match current Art. 12. Cite the current numbering, or give both, so an exam-savvy commenter can't catch a stale reference.
