## Suspended provisions — how this week handles them

Circular 1873 (30 Dec 2023) lists the FFAR provisions FIFA suspended worldwide:

> "The service fee cap (article 15 paragraphs 1-4) · The rules concerning service fee payments (article 14 paragraphs 6, 8 and 11) · The client pays rule (article 14 paragraphs 2 and 10) · The rules regarding the timing of service fee payments (article 14 paragraphs 7 and 12) · The prohibition of double representation (article 12 paragraphs 8-10) · The reporting obligations (article 16 paragraphs 2 h), j), k) and 4) · The rules regarding disclosure and publication (article 19) · The submission rule (article 4 paragraph 2; article 16 paragraph 2 b); article 3 paragraphs 2 c) and d); article 20; and article 21) · The rule that service fee payments must be made via the FIFA Clearing House (article 14 paragraph 13)"

> "on 30 December 2023 the Bureau of the Council approved the worldwide temporary suspension of the FFAR rules affected by the above-mentioned German court decision, until the European Court of Justice renders a final decision in the pending procedures concerning the FFAR"

Study Materials (Jan 2026), Introduction: "Please note that the Exam will not feature the suspended provisions of the FFAR, as per FIFA circular no. 1873."

**Current status (28 Sep 2026):** the CJEU gave judgment on 16 Jul 2026 (C-209/23). FIFA's statement: the court "confirmed that key elements of the FFAR, i.e., rules such as the licensing requirement for agents, the service fee cap, the prohibition of multiple representation, the client pays rule and the pro rata payment rule, can be justified" and "We will continue to carefully analyse the full judgment and its practical implications." I found **no FIFA circular reinstating the suspended provisions** on the inside.fifa.com agents pages (the latest agents news item is the 16 Jul statement). So: days 1–6 test only provisions NOT on the suspended list; day 7 is ABOUT the suspension and is phrased as what Circular 1873 said, which stays true whatever FIFA does next. The Page/group copy says the cap "has been suspended since 2023" — re-check if FIFA issues a new circular. (FFAR numbering is the same in the 2022 and 2025 editions — only art. 6 changed — so the circular's article numbers map onto the current text.)

Things deliberately NOT used: licence-fee amounts (USD 600 / 300 come from Circular 1874 and are "communicated annually by circular", art. 7 par. 2); the exam-fee amount (shown only on the Platform); art. 17 par. 3 b) (refers to the Disciplinary Committee — art. 21 is suspended, and FIFA's own general-secretariat decisions say it cannot currently refer those cases); dual representation (art. 12 par. 8–10, suspended).

## Day 1 — The exam (answer A)

**Sources:** Exam Rules (Jan 2026) art. 7 par. 1; Study Materials (Jan 2026) Introduction; Information on the Licensing Process (Jan 2026) Q8.

- Keys A (electronic only, no printed copies) — Exam Rules art. 7 par. 1: "Candidates may bring and use any of the following Allowed Items during the Exam Session after consulting the Invigilators during the pre-Exam requirements verification (exhaustive list): … c) The Study Materials (accessed only electronically on the approved Devices)". Study Materials, Introduction: "during the Exam, only the use of the Study Materials available within the Exam interface will be allowed. Therefore, no printed (hard) copies of the Study Materials may be used while taking the Exam."
- Kills B (printed copy): same sentence — "no printed (hard) copies of the Study Materials may be used".
- Kills C (any website, as long as no person helps): the allowed-items list is "exhaustive" and limits reference material to the Study Materials in the exam interface; Information Q8: "Candidates may consult materials on the Platform (i.e. it will be an electronic open-book Exam), but may not consult any other person by any means whatsoever."
- Kills D (closed-book): Information Q8 — "it will be an electronic open-book Exam".
- Caveat recorded in the evening post: art. 7 par. 1 d) also allows "A blank sheet of paper and a pen" (destroyed afterwards) — scratch paper, not reference material, so it doesn't make A wrong; the stem asks how the Study Materials may be consulted.

## Day 2 — Representation agreements (answer C)

**Source:** FFAR 2025, art. 12 par. 3, 4, 5; RSTP (July 2025) art. 18 par. 2.

- Keys C — art. 12 par. 3: "A Representation Agreement concluded between an Individual and a Football Agent may not exceed two years. This term may be extended by a new Representation Agreement only. Any automatic renewal provision, or any other provision that purports to extend any term of the Representation Agreement beyond the maximum period, shall be null and void." (Definitions: "Individual: player or coach.")
- Kills A (auto-renewal): same paragraph — "Any automatic renewal provision … shall be null and void."
- Kills B (five years): the five-year figure is the RSTP limit on employment contracts — RSTP art. 18 par. 2: "the maximum length of a contract shall be five years" — not the agent-agreement limit, which art. 12 par. 3 sets at two.
- Kills D (no maximum if advised): art. 12 par. 5 — "A Representation Agreement concluded between an Engaging Entity or Releasing Entity and a Football Agent is not subject to a maximum duration" (clubs, not players); the legal-advice duty in art. 12 par. 4 ("inform the Individual in writing that they should consider taking independent legal advice") is separate and does not lift par. 3.
- Scope note: art. 3 par. 3 lets national associations adopt "stricter measures"; the stem pins "Under the FFAR".

## Day 3 — Service fees (answer D)

**Source:** FFAR 2025, art. 14 par. 5; art. 12 par. 1, 7, 11. (Art. 14 par. 5 is not on the Circular 1873 list.)

- Keys D — art. 14 par. 5: "A Football Agent is entitled to receive a service fee only if the fee corresponds to the services stipulated in advance in a Representation Agreement, and the Representation Agreement is in force at the time at which the relevant Football Agent Services are performed."
- Kills A (registration-period timing): no such condition in art. 14 par. 5; the only registration-period payment rule (art. 14 par. 6) is suspended and concerns WHEN payment is made, not entitlement.
- Kills B (verbal confirmation afterwards): "stipulated in advance in a Representation Agreement"; and a Representation Agreement is "a written agreement" (Definitions) that is "valid only if it contains … c) The amount of the service fee due to the Football Agent" (art. 12 par. 7).
- Kills C (name on the transfer agreement suffices): art. 12 par. 1 — "A Football Agent may only perform Football Agent Services for a Client after having entered into a written Representation Agreement with that Client." The name/licence/signature requirement is art. 12 par. 11 ("shall specify the Football Agent's name, their Client, their FIFA licence number and their signature") — a disclosure duty, not a source of entitlement.

## Day 4 — Conflicts of interest (answer B)

**Source:** FFAR 2025, art. 11 par. 4 a); art. 18 par. 2 f) and i); art. 5 par. 1 a) v); Definitions.

- Keys B — art. 11 par. 4: "The following natural or legal persons may not have an Interest in any affairs of a Football Agent or their Agency: a) Clients". Art. 18 par. 2: "Clients (and their officials, when applicable) may not engage, or attempt to engage, in the following conduct: … f) Have an Interest in an Agency or the affairs of a Football Agent, in accordance with article 11 paragraph 4 of these Regulations". Definitions: "Client: a member association, club, player, coach, or Single-Entity League that may engage a Football Agent"; "Interest: (i) any beneficial ownership of a legal person through which the relevant activities of those entities are conducted …".
- Kills A (allowed if disclosed): the prohibition in art. 11 par. 4 has no disclosure exception.
- Kills C (allowed with FIFA general-secretariat approval): art. 11 par. 4 provides no approval route — it is a flat prohibition; nor does art. 26 ("matters not provided for") apply, since the matter is provided for. And "any beneficial ownership" means the 20% size is irrelevant.
- Kills D (players' consent): no consent exception exists in art. 11 par. 4 or art. 18 par. 2.
- Reverse direction mentioned in the answer — art. 5 par. 1 a) v): an applicant must "not hold, either personally or through their Agency, any Interest in a club, academy, league or Single-Entity League".

## Day 5 — Minors (answer B)

**Source:** FFAR 2025, art. 13 par. 1, 2, 4. (Art. 13 is not on the Circular 1873 list.)

- Keys B — art. 13 par. 1: "An Approach (and/or any subsequent execution of a Representation Agreement) to a minor or their legal guardian in relation to any Football Agent Services may only be made no more than six months before the minor reaches the age where they may sign their first professional contract in accordance with the law applicable in the country or territory where the minor will be employed. This Approach may only be made once prior written consent has been obtained from the minor's legal guardian."
- Kills A (any age with consent): same paragraph — the six-month limit applies in addition to consent.
- Kills C (only at 18): the age is "the age where they may sign their first professional contract in accordance with the law applicable", not 18.
- Kills D (twelve months before 16, club consent): the window is six months, keyed to national law, and the consent required is the "legal guardian's", not a club's.
- Also cited in the answer — art. 13 par. 2: an agent who wishes to represent a minor "shall first successfully complete the designated CPD course on minors"; art. 13 par. 4: "Any violation of paragraph 1 shall be sanctioned, at a minimum, with a fine and a suspension of a Football Agent licence of up to two years."
- Pinning: the stem asks "under the FFAR"; the national-law element is the age, which the correct option expresses generically ("they may sign a first pro contract").

## Day 6 — Keeping the licence (answer D)

**Source:** FFAR 2025, art. 17 par. 1 b), par. 4; art. 16 par. 2 f). (Art. 17 and art. 16 par. 2 f) are not on the Circular 1873 list; the FIFA general-secretariat decisions reproduced in the Study Materials still apply art. 17 par. 1 provisional suspensions.)

- Keys D — art. 17 par. 1: "If a Football Agent fails to: … b) pay the annual licence fee to FIFA within the deadline stipulated on the Platform; … their licence shall automatically be provisionally suspended." Art. 17 par. 4: "a) the FIFA general secretariat will notify the Football Agent of their non-compliance and of the automatic provisional suspension; and b) if the Football Agent fails to rectify their non-compliance within 60 days of their licence being automatically provisionally suspended, their licence shall be withdrawn."
- Kills A (stays active pending a Disciplinary Committee hearing): the suspension is "automatic"; the Disciplinary Committee route in art. 17 par. 3 applies only to eligibility failures (par. 1 a)), not to fee non-payment.
- Kills B (12-month grace, then re-sit): the period is 60 days, and the consequence is withdrawal, not a re-sit.
- Kills C (withdrawn immediately, 24-month wait): withdrawal comes only after the 60 days; the FFAR's 24-month periods (art. 5 par. 1 b), unlicensed activity before applying; art. 15 par. 3, suspended) are unrelated.
- Duty itself — art. 16 par. 2 f): an agent shall "pay an annual licence fee to FIFA within the deadline stipulated on the Platform".

## Day 7 — The 2023 suspension (answer C)

**Source:** FIFA Circular no. 1873 (30 Dec 2023); Study Materials (Jan 2026) Introduction; FIFA statement 16 Jul 2026.

- Keys C — Circular 1873: "The Injunction requests FIFA to suspend the application and enforcement of the certain provisions of the FFAR: • The service fee cap (article 15 paragraphs 1-4) …" and "the Bureau of the Council approved the worldwide temporary suspension of the FFAR rules affected by the above-mentioned German court decision, until the European Court of Justice renders a final decision in the pending procedures concerning the FFAR".
- Kills A (two-year term, art. 12 par. 3), B (exam requirement, art. 4 par. 1 c) / art. 6) and D (CPD, art. 9): none appears in the circular's list (quoted in full above). The only art. 4 item suspended is art. 4 par. 2 (the "submission rule"), not the exam requirement in par. 1.
- Status line in the answer — FIFA 16 Jul 2026: "We will continue to carefully analyse the full judgment and its practical implications." Study Materials: "the Exam will not feature the suspended provisions of the FFAR, as per FIFA circular no. 1873."
