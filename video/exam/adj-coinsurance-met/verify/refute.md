I checked the video against ISO's standard commercial property form. The keyed answer is right and nothing on screen teaches something wrong.

**Source:** ISO form CP 00 10 10 12, Coinsurance condition (F.1), https://www.propertyinsurancecoveragelaw.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/CP-00-10-10-12-Building-and-Personal-Property-Coverage-Form.pdf

- When the penalty applies: *"We will not pay the full amount of any loss if the value of Covered Property at the time of loss times the Coinsurance percentage shown for it in the Declarations is greater than the Limit of Insurance for the property."*
- The steps: *"(2) Divide the Limit of Insurance of the property by the figure determined in Step (1); (3) Multiply the total amount of loss, before the application of any deductible, by the figure determined in Step (2); and (4) Subtract the deductible from the figure determined in Step (3). We will pay the amount determined in Step (4) or the Limit of Insurance, whichever is less."*
- The form's own example of a limit that meets the requirement: *"the Limit of Insurance in this example is adequate, and no penalty applies. We will pay no more than $39,750 ($40,000 amount of loss minus the deductible of $250)."*

**The numbers, recomputed:**
- **Required insurance:** $500,000 × 90% = $450,000. The limit is $450,000, which is not *greater than* the requirement, so there's no penalty. "450 ÷ 450", "MET — NO PENALTY" and "× 1" are all correct.
- **Payment:** $80,000 × 1 − $2,500 = **$77,500**, well under the $450,000 limit. The keyed answer C is correct.

**The wrong answers:**
- **A) $69,500:** $80,000 × (450,000 ÷ 500,000) = $72,000, minus $2,500 = $69,500. That's what you get by dividing by the full value instead of the 90% requirement, so "THE TRAP" line is accurate. It also subtracts the deductible after the penalty, which matches step 4.
- **B) $72,000:** divides by the full value and skips the deductible. Wrong.
- **D) $80,000:** skips the deductible. Wrong.
- No wrong answer is also correct. The other order some older forms use (deductible first) gives $69,750, which isn't an option, so it can't confuse anyone.

**Other checks:**
- The figures are labelled "example".
- The order on screen (requirement, then payment, then the trap) follows the form's steps.
- "Rules vary by state" is present.
- "Building value at the time of loss" matches the form's "value of Covered Property at the time of loss".

**Style nit (doesn't change the verdict):** "THE TRAP" only explains A. That's fine for a short video, but a viewer who picked B or D gets no explanation.
