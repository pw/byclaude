**Working:**

1. **How much insurance the policy needs to carry:** coinsurance percentage × value at the time of loss = 90% × $500,000 = **$450,000**.
2. **How much it carries:** the limit is **$450,000**, so the requirement is exactly met. There is no coinsurance penalty.
3. **Payment:** because the requirement is met, the insurer pays the full loss minus the deductible. $80,000 − $2,500 = **$77,500**. That is under the $450,000 limit, so the limit doesn't cap it.

This follows the ISO CP 00 10 coinsurance condition. The requirement is measured against value at the time of loss, which is how the question states it. If the limit is at least the required amount, the loss is adjusted as if there were no coinsurance clause, and only the deductible comes off. When the requirement isn't met, ISO applies the deductible *after* the penalty ratio. That order doesn't matter here because the ratio is 1.
