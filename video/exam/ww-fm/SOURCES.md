# ww-fm — sources

Film: the food-to-microorganism (F/M) ratio for an activated sludge aeration tank, set up the way the operator
formula sheet writes it. Original question — not taken from any exam, vendor item bank or prep company. All figures
in the question are illustrative (labelled `example` on the question card); the end card says
`Check your state's exam and formula sheet.`

## Rules and their authority

1. **The F/M formula: BOD pounds per day divided by MLVSS pounds.** The ABC Formula/Conversion Table for the
   Wastewater Treatment, Collection, Industrial Waste & Wastewater Laboratory exams, Copyright © 2023 by Water
   Professionals International (the certifier formerly named the Association of Boards of Certification, ABC),
   as distributed by the New York Water Environment Association for NY operator exams.
   URL: https://nywea.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/WWT-FCT_081023_with-Pie-Wheels.pdf (page 3)
   > "Food/Microorganism Ratio = BOD5 , lb/day / MLVSS, lb"  (written as a fraction on the sheet)
   Abbreviations list, same sheet:
   > "MG .............. million US gallons" · "MGD ............ million US gallons per day" ·
   > "MLVSS ........ mixed liquor volatile suspended solids" · "F/M ratio ...... food to microorganism ratio"

2. **The two pounds formulas (flow for the food, tank volume for the microorganisms).** Same sheet, page 4:
   > "Loading Rate, lb/day* = (Flow, MGD)(Concentration, mg/L)(8.34 lb/gal)"
   > "Mass, lb* = (Volume, MG)(Concentration, mg/L)(8.34 lb/gal)"

3. **The rename (why the film says "formula sheet", not a vendor name).** Water Professionals International:
   URL: https://immerse.gowpi.org/2023/07/wpis-impactful-rebrand-receives-top-association-industry-award/
   > "After a half-century of being known as the Association of Boards of Certification, those present who were
   > eligible to vote, unanimously voted to begin the process of renaming ABC, ultimately deciding on the name
   > 'Water Professionals International.'"

4. **State operator training says the same: the food is the BOD entering the aeration tank (primary effluent), the
   microorganisms are MLVSS pounds in the aeration tank.** Pennsylvania DEP, Wastewater Treatment Plant Operator
   Certification Training, Module 16 (The Activated Sludge Process, Part II), p. 1-21.
   URL: https://files.dep.state.pa.us/water/bsdw/operatorcertification/TrainingModules/ww16_sludge_2_wb.pdf
   > "The food-to-microorganism (F/M) ratio is a measurement of the amount of incoming food (lbs of Influent BOD)
   > divided by the pounds of microorganisms in your system."
   > "Influent BOD (mg/l) concentration into your aeration tank." · "Mixed Liquor Volatile Suspended Solids
   > Concentration (mg/l)"
   > "Primary clarifier effluent BOD (same as influent to the aeration tank) = 200 mg/l"
   Same module, p. 1-33: "F/M = influent BOD to the activated sludge system, lb/day / MLVSS, lb"; its worked example
   uses "MLVSS/MLSS = 0.80" (the same ratio as this film's 2,400 / 3,000).

5. **A second and third state sheet, both MLVSS (so the MLSS option is wrong under every sheet checked).**
   - Kentucky Energy & Environment Cabinet, Wastewater Treatment Conversion Factors and Formulas:
     https://eec.ky.gov/Environmental-Protection/Compliance-Assistance/operator-certification-program/Documents/WWConversionTable[1].pdf
     > "FM Ratio = Flow, MGD x BOD, mg/l x 8.34 / Lbs MLVSS in Aeration Tank"
     > "or = BOD, mg/l x Flow, MGD x 8.34 / MLVSS, mg/l x Vol in Aeration Tank, MG x 8.34"
   - Indiana Department of Environmental Management, Formula Sheet for all Wastewater Operator Exams (Revised 3/17),
     formula F064: https://www.in.gov/idem/cleanwater/files/wastewater_cert_study_guide_formula_sheet.pdf
     > "F/M = lbs BOD/day to aeration tank / lbs of MLVSS under aeration"

Edition note: the 2023 WPI sheet, the older ABC sheets (2009/2018) and the KY/IN/PA state materials all define F/M
with MLVSS and the 8.34 lb/gal pounds formula; nothing on this film changes between them.

## Worked example (illustrative figures)

Given (on the question card): aeration tank volume 0.5 MG · primary effluent into the tank 1.5 MGD at BOD 160 mg/L ·
MLSS 3,000 mg/L · MLVSS 2,400 mg/L. (MLVSS / MLSS = 2,400 / 3,000 = 0.80.)

Food, lb/day = flow × BOD × 8.34 = 1.5 × 160 × 8.34 = 240 × 8.34 = 2,001.6 lb/day
Microorganisms, lb = volume × MLVSS × 8.34 = 0.5 × 2,400 × 8.34 = 1,200 × 8.34 = 10,008 lb
F/M = 2,001.6 ÷ 10,008 = 0.20 (exactly 0.2)

Shortcut shown on screen: the 8.34 appears top and bottom and cancels, so F/M = 240 ÷ 1,200 = 0.20.

Answer **C) 0.20**.

The distractors, each wrong for a stated reason:
- A) 0.07 — divides the concentrations only and drops flow and volume: 160 ÷ 2,400 = 0.0667, about 0.07.
- B) 0.16 — uses MLSS instead of MLVSS: 0.5 × 3,000 × 8.34 = 12,510 lb; 2,001.6 ÷ 12,510 = 0.16 (240 ÷ 1,500).
  This is the trap line on screen: "B) 0.16 uses MLSS. F/M divides by MLVSS, the volatile solids used to measure
  the microorganisms."
- D) 5.00 — the ratio upside down (M/F): 10,008 ÷ 2,001.6 = 5.00.

Other numbers on screen: countdown ring digits 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 (a five-second pause timer, not data).
