**Working it through**

1. **Depreciation (straight-line):** the roof is 8 years into a 20-year life, so it is 8/20 = 40% depreciated. 40% × $12,000 = **$4,800**.
2. **Actual cash value (ACV):** replacement cost minus depreciation. $12,000 − $4,800 = **$7,200**.
3. **Deductible:** it is taken from the first (ACV) payment. $7,200 − $1,000 = **$6,200**.
4. **Holdback:** the $4,800 of depreciation is recoverable. The insurer pays it once the roof is actually replaced. Total paid would then be $6,200 + $4,800 = $11,000, which equals full replacement cost less the deductible.

Under the ISO HO-3 replacement-cost condition, the insurer pays only ACV until repair or replacement is done. The one exception is a loss under $2,500, and this $12,000 loss doesn't qualify. So the first payment is $6,200.
