# fpm-reheat — sources

Film: reheating cooked-and-cooled TCS food for hot holding. Original question — not taken from any exam, vendor
item bank or prep company. The end card says `Based on the FDA Food Code; your local code may differ.`

Authority: the FDA Food Code, in BOTH the 2022 edition (the one most state/local codes adopt today) and the 2026
edition (released by FDA 2026-09-17). The rules below are the same in both. The only wording change in 3-403.11
is that 2026 spells numbers out ("two minutes", "two hours" where 2022 has "2 minutes", "2 hours").

- 2022: https://www.fda.gov/media/164194/download (FDA Food Code 2022, January 18, 2023 version) — page Chapter 3 - 24/25
- 2026: https://www.fda.gov/media/194741/download (FDA Food Code 2026) — page 68
- Edition pages: https://www.fda.gov/food/fda-food-code/food-code-2022 · https://www.fda.gov/food/fda-food-code/food-code-2026

## Rules and their authority (verbatim, 2022 text; 2026 identical except as noted)

1. **The answer: food cooked, cooled and reheated for hot holding → all parts to 165°F for 15 seconds.**
   Food Code 3-403.11(A):
   > "(A) Except as specified under ¶¶ (B) and (C) and in ¶ (E) of this section, TIME/TEMPERATURE CONTROL FOR SAFETY
   > FOOD that is cooked, cooled, and reheated for hot holding shall be reheated so that all parts of the FOOD reach a
   > temperature of at least 74°C (165°F) for 15 seconds. P"

2. **The time limit: from 41°F up to 165°F in no more than 2 hours.** Food Code 3-403.11(D):
   > "(D) Reheating for hot holding as specified under ¶¶ (A) - (C) of this section shall be done rapidly and the time
   > the food is between 5ºC (41ºF) and the temperatures specified under ¶¶ (A) - (C) of this section may not exceed
   > 2 hours. P"   (2026: "may not exceed two hours.")

3. **After reheating, hold hot at 135°F or above.** Food Code 3-501.16(A)(1):
   > "TIME/TEMPERATURE CONTROL FOR SAFETY FOOD shall be maintained: (1) At 57°C (135°F) or above, except that roasts
   > cooked to a temperature and for a time specified in ¶ 3-401.11(B) or reheated as specified in ¶ 3-403.11(E) may
   > be held at a temperature of 54°C (130°F) or above; P or (2) At 5°C (41°F) or less. P"

4. **Why the question pins each condition** (the exceptions that would change the answer):
   - Microwave → 3-403.11(B): "reheated in a microwave oven for hot holding shall be reheated so that all parts of
     the FOOD reach a temperature of at least 74°C (165°F) and the FOOD is rotated or stirred, covered, and allowed to
     stand covered for 2 minutes after reheating." (no "15 seconds") → the question says **on the stove**.
   - Factory-packaged food → 3-403.11(C): "READY-TO-EAT TIME/TEMPERATURE CONTROL FOR SAFETY FOOD that has been
     commercially processed and PACKAGED in a FOOD PROCESSING PLANT ... shall be heated to a temperature of at least
     57°C (135°F) when being reheated for hot holding." → the question says **your kitchen cooked** the chili.
   - Roasts → 3-403.11(E): "Remaining unsliced portions of MEAT roasts ... may be reheated for hot holding using the
     oven parameters ... specified under ¶ 3-401.11(B)." → the food is **chili**, not a roast.
   - Immediate service → 3-403.10: "Cooked and refrigerated FOOD that is prepared for immediate service in response
     to an individual CONSUMER order, such as a roast beef sandwich au jus, may be served at any temperature."
     → the question says reheated **for hot holding** (the Code's own words).

5. **Where the distractor temperatures come from** (each is a real Food Code number for a DIFFERENT job):
   - 135°F — hot holding (3-501.16(A)(1), quoted above) and the reheat for commercially processed, packaged RTE food
     (3-403.11(C), quoted above). Wrong here: the chili was cooked and cooled in-house.
   - 145°F — cooking, 3-401.11(A)(1): "63°C (145°F) or above for 15 seconds for: ... FISH and INTACT MEAT ..."
     Wrong here: that is a cooking temperature for raw fish/intact meat, not a reheat.
   - 155°F — cooking, 3-401.11(A)(2): "68°C (155°F) for 17 seconds ... for RATITES, and nonINTACT MEATS; ... if they
     are COMMINUTED ..." Wrong here: a cooking temperature (and for 17 seconds, not 15), not a reheat.

## Worked answer (as drawn on the thermometer)

- 41°F — the chili comes out of the walk-in (cold holding is 41°F or less, 3-501.16(A)(2)).
- the climb: 41°F → 165°F must take 2 hours max (3-403.11(D)).
- 165°F — all parts, at least, for 15 seconds (3-403.11(A))  → answer **D) 165°F for 15 seconds**
- 135°F — then hold hot at or above (3-501.16(A)(1)).
- Cite on screen: FOOD CODE 3-403.11.

Distractors: A) 135°F for 15 seconds · B) 145°F for 15 seconds · C) 155°F for 15 seconds — see 5 above.
Trap line on screen: "A) 135°F is for holding, not reheating. Food you cooked and cooled goes to 165°F."

Other numbers on screen: countdown ring digits 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 (a five-second pause timer, not data).
Temperature scale on the drawn thermometer is illustrative (linear between 41°F and 165°F).
