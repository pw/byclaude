**Keyed answer D (165°F for 15 seconds): correct.** §3-403.11(A): *"TIME/TEMPERATURE CONTROL FOR SAFETY FOOD that is cooked, cooled, and reheated for hot holding shall be reheated so that all parts of the FOOD reach a temperature of at least 74°C (165°F) for 15 seconds."* The screen says "every part" and "all parts, at least," which matches the code's wording. The chili is reheated on a stove, not in a microwave, so the 15-second rule in ¶(A) applies. The microwave rule in ¶(B) (165°F, covered, 2-minute stand) doesn't come into it.

**"2 hours max" / "41°F out of the cooler": correct.** §3-403.11(D): *"the time the food is between 5ºC (41ºF) and the temperatures specified under ¶¶ (A) - (C) of this section may not exceed 2 hours."* The on-screen order (41°F, then a climb of 2 hours max, then 165°F) follows the rule.

**"135°F, then hold hot, at or above": correct.** §3-501.16(A)(1): *"At 57°C (135°F) or above."* The code lets roasts be held at 130°F, but that exception has nothing to do with chili.
