August 2, 1947, over the Andes. The airliner Star Dust, flying from Buenos Aires to Santiago, sent its last message in Morse code: an arrival time four minutes away, then one word, STENDEC. The radio operator in Santiago asked again, and the same word came back twice more. The plane never arrived; its wreckage was found on Mount Tupungato in 1998. The crash itself is broadly understood: a descent in thick cloud while still over the mountains. No one aboard survived. The word has never been explained. Theories include a scrambled "descent" or a misheard Morse sign-off, but none has been confirmed.

Still unsolved.
