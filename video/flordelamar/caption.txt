In 1511, in the Strait of Malacca, the Portuguese carrack Flor de la Mar set sail carrying the treasure taken from the Sultan of Malacca's palace. Off the coast of Sumatra, a storm drove her aground. Her commander, Afonso de Albuquerque, escaped on a raft, and hundreds of men were lost. Portugal, Malaysia and Indonesia have all claimed what she carried. Treasure hunters have announced finds, but none has been confirmed. Five centuries on, the ship and her treasure have never been found.
