In the late 1990s, road builders at Lomas de Tacamichapa, in Veracruz, Mexico, found a serpentine slab in a pile of debris, near the old Olmec centre of San Lorenzo. Its face carries 62 carved signs, set in horizontal rows, some of them repeated. In 2006, researchers writing in Science proposed it as the oldest writing in the Americas, from around 900 BCE, dated by the pottery found with it. No other text in its script has ever been found. Some scholars doubt it is writing at all; others question its context, since it was not found in an excavation. It has never been deciphered.
