Virginia, 1885. A pamphlet went on sale telling of treasure buried in Bedford County. It said a man named Thomas Beale had left a locked iron box with an innkeeper in 1822. Inside were three messages written only in numbers. The second was decoded using the Declaration of Independence, and describes gold, silver, and jewels. No record has ever confirmed Beale existed, and many researchers think the story is a hoax. The other two messages have never been decoded.

Still unsolved.
