January 30, 1948, over the Atlantic. The British airliner Star Tiger was flying through the night from the Azores to Bermuda, with 31 people aboard. Just after 3 a.m., she asked Bermuda for a radio bearing. Nothing more was heard. The search found no wreckage, and no one aboard was ever seen again. The official inquiry wrote that no more baffling problem had ever been presented for investigation. Theories include fuel running out, and sabotage. The wreck has never been found.

Still unsolved.
