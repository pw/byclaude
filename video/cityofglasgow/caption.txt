On 1 March 1854, the Inman Line steamship City of Glasgow left Liverpool for Philadelphia with more than 400 people aboard. Most were emigrants travelling in steerage. She never arrived, and no word of her ever came. That month her sister ship, the City of Manchester, met many icebergs and large fields of ice, and other ships reported the worst ice in years; ice is one theory, never proven. In May, her owners accepted she was lost. No one aboard was ever seen again. Wreckage was reported more than once, but none was ever proven to be hers, and her wreck has never been found.

Still unsolved.
