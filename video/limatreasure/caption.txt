In 1820, the story goes, the riches of Lima, Peru, were loaded onto one ship to keep them from revolution. Among them, it is said, was a life-size statue of the Virgin in solid gold. Its captain is said to have sailed instead for Cocos Island, off Costa Rica, and buried it there. More than 300 expeditions have searched the island since, and none found the treasure. August Gissler searched there from 1889 to 1908 and found only a few gold coins. Some historians doubt the story ever happened, and Costa Rica now forbids treasure hunting on the island. The gold, if it existed, has never been found.
