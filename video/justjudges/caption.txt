On the night of 10 April 1934, two panels of Van Eyck's Ghent Altarpiece were taken from Saint Bavo's Cathedral in Ghent, Belgium. One was the Just Judges. The other was Saint John the Baptist. Thirteen ransom letters followed to the Bishop of Ghent, signed only D.U.A., asking one million Belgian francs. To prove it, the writer returned Saint John through the luggage depot at Brussels North station. The Just Judges never came back. The case file is still open, and a copy hangs in its place.

Still unsolved.
