In 1505, in the great council hall of the Palazzo Vecchio in Florence, Leonardo da Vinci began work on a mural of the Battle of Anghiari. He left it unfinished. Decades later, Giorgio Vasari remade the hall and painted his own battle scenes on its walls. High on one of them, on a small green flag, are two words: "Cerca trova", seek and you shall find. Some read them as a clue that Leonardo's work survives behind a newer wall. In 2011, a team slipped a camera behind the fresco and reported a gap and black pigment like Leonardo's; others disputed it, and the search was halted in 2012. Some historians argue Leonardo never painted that wall at all.

Still unsolved.
