December 1, 1946, Bennington, Vermont. Paula Jean Welden, an 18-year-old college student, left campus to go hiking. She wore a red coat, jeans, and sneakers. Around 4 p.m., hikers on the Long Trail near Glastenbury Mountain spoke with her. No one is known to have seen her again. Hundreds searched the woods. No trace of her was ever found. Vermont had no state police; her case led to the creation of one in 1947.

Still unsolved.
