November 24, 1971, Portland, Oregon. A man who called himself Dan Cooper boarded a short flight to Seattle. In the air, he passed a note saying he had a bomb. He asked for $200,000 and four parachutes. In Seattle, the passengers were released and the money was delivered. Then, somewhere over southwest Washington, the rear stairs came down. When the plane landed in Reno, he was gone. In 1980, a boy found some of the money on a bank of the Columbia River. The man has never been identified.

Still unsolved.
