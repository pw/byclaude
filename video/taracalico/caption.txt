September 20, 1988, Belen, New Mexico. Nineteen-year-old Tara Calico left home at 9:30 a.m. for a bike ride on Highway 47. She asked her mother to pick her up if she wasn't home by noon. Witnesses saw an older pickup truck following close behind her. No one saw what happened next. Neither Tara nor her bicycle was ever found. In 1989, a Polaroid found in Florida was thought by some to show her; its identification was never established. In 2023 the case went to prosecutors. No one has ever been charged.
