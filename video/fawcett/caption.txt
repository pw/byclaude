1925, Mato Grosso, Brazil. Percy Fawcett, his son Jack and Raleigh Rimell went into the forest looking for a lost city he called Z. He had left word that if they did not return, no rescue should be sent. On May 29 he wrote to his wife from Dead Horse Camp: "You need have no fear of any failure." It was the last word from any of them. Searchers went in anyway. In 1951, bones said to be his were examined in London and found not to be his. None of the three was ever found.

Still unsolved.
