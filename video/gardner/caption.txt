18 March 1990, Boston. In the early hours, two men dressed as police officers talked their way into the Isabella Stewart Gardner Museum. About 81 minutes later they left with 13 works, among them Vermeer's The Concert and The Storm on the Sea of Galilee, Rembrandt's only known seascape. The museum has chosen to leave the empty frames in place, in the hope the works will one day come home. None of the works has ever been recovered, and no one has ever been charged. The reward for information leading to their safe return is $10 million.

Still unsolved.
