In 1746, weeks after the Battle of Culloden, two French ships reached Scotland's west coast with gold for the Jacobite cause. The rising was already lost. Seven caskets of gold came ashore, and one was stolen almost at once. The other six were carried inland and hidden near Loch Arkaig, and only a few men knew where. Some of it later reached Bonnie Prince Charlie in France, and his followers accused one another over the rest. French gold coins turned up in the woods nearby in the 1850s. Some say the rest was gone long ago. If any of it is still there, it has never been found.
