July 1897, England. The composer Edward Elgar slipped a short note into his wife's letter to the Penny family, meant for a young friend, Dora. It held no words, only 87 small curved marks in three lines. Dora could make nothing of it and kept it for about forty years. In 1937 she printed a copy in her memoir. The original is now thought lost. Many have claimed to break it, and no solution has ever been generally accepted.

Still unsolved.
