December 1872, the Atlantic Ocean between the Azores and Portugal. The brigantine Mary Celeste was found drifting with no one aboard. She was still seaworthy, her cargo of 1,701 barrels of alcohol was almost untouched, and she had plenty of food and water. Ten people had sailed on her: the captain, his wife, their two-year-old daughter and seven crew. The ship's only boat was gone. Her last log entry, nine days earlier, put her off the Azores. Not one of the ten was ever seen again.

Still unsolved.
