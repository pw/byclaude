December 1928, Buenos Aires. The Danish training ship København, a five-masted barque and then the largest sailing ship in the world, set out for Australia. 60 people were aboard, 45 of them cadets. Eight days out, she radioed a passing steamer that all was well. When it called her again, she did not answer. A search across the South Atlantic lasted months and found nothing. Reported sightings were never confirmed, and no one aboard was ever seen again.

Still unsolved.
