In May 1968, the US nuclear submarine Scorpion was crossing the Atlantic, heading home to Norfolk, Virginia. Her last message came on 21 May, and she was due on 27 May. She never arrived, and she was lost with all 99 men aboard. In late October 1968, the research ship Mizar found the wreck about 400 nautical miles southwest of the Azores, almost two miles down. A Navy court of inquiry said the certain cause of the loss could not be ascertained. An explosion, a torpedo and a battery fault have all been proposed, but none has been proven.
