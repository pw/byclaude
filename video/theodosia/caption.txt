December 31, 1812, Georgetown, South Carolina. The schooner Patriot sailed for New York. Aboard was Theodosia Burr Alston, Aaron Burr's daughter and the wife of South Carolina's governor. Neither the ship nor anyone aboard was ever found. British ships logged a storm off the Carolina coast two days later. Stories of pirates, wreckers and confessions followed, and none was ever proven. A woman's portrait found on the Outer Banks in 1869 has never been confirmed as hers.

Still unsolved.
