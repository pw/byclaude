# @smallbusinessinbox — slate 1 (six films)

**The account:** the inside of a small-business owner's phone. Stylists, nail techs, groomers,
photographers, fence and landscape guys. Non-promotional: no product, no pitch, no link. The owner
watching should think *that is my phone at 11pm* and send it to someone who owns a shop.

**The form:** motion design, code-drawn. A messaging thread in our OWN visual language (not a copy
of any real app), a compose box where the owner types and deletes drafts, timestamps, a status bar.
No voice. The words on screen ARE the script; the deleted drafts are the owner's inner life. Sound
is room tone, key taps, deletions, notification chimes, a drone for unease and a warm pad for the turn.

**Honesty:** every film is a WRITTEN COMPOSITE. No real business, person, town or platform is named.
The end card says so in small type: `A written composite. Not a real business or client.` The bio and
pinned post say it too (Patrick). Customers are people, never punchlines — the humour lives in the
situation and in the owner's deleted drafts, never in mocking a client. The owner is competent and
kind; the turn is always something the owner DOES, not a lesson.

Names used below are invented. Keep them first-name-only and ordinary.

## 1. `quick-question` — the stylist, 11:48 PM  (builds the kit; posts first)
- 11:48 PM. Incoming, from **Kayla**: `hi!! quick question`
- Then a cascade, one bubble at a time, faster: `how much are highlights` · `like just some not full` ·
  `is that with a cut` · `my friend said you did hers for 90?` · `also do you take walk ins`
- Owner types a long, careful paragraph about pricing depending on length and time. Deletes it.
  Types a shorter one. Deletes it. Sends: `Partial highlights start at $140. Happy to do a free consult!`
- `Seen 11:52 PM`. Nothing. The thread sits.
- Time jump: status bar clock rolls to **7:02 AM**. New thread, new name, **Dee**: `hi!! quick question`
- The owner's thumb hovers. Small type rises: `quick questions this week: 23`
- Card.

## 2. `no-show` — the nail tech, 9:00 AM
- 8:56 AM, owner sends to **Brianna**: `Morning! All set up for you at 9.`
- The thread goes quiet. The status-bar clock advances; timestamps of nothing. 9:04. 9:15. 9:30.

- Card.

## 3. `friend-price` — the photographer
- Incoming from **Jess**, a name with no recent history in the thread (old bubbles from years ago faded
  above: `happy birthday!!`): `HEYYY long time!!` · `I'm getting married!!!` ·
  `would you do our photos? friend price lol`
- The owner opens a note (drawn as a second surface sliding up). The math builds, one line at a time:
  `shooting 8 hrs` · `driving 2 hrs` · `editing 30 hrs` · `gallery + delivery 1 hr` · `= 41 hrs`.
  Then: `friend price? $500` → `= $12.19 / hr` (and it is: 500 / 41 = 12.195…). Then, quieter:
  `before gear, insurance, taxes`.
- Owner drafts: `Congrats!! My wedding packages start at` — deletes.
  Drafts: `Honestly I can't really discount` — deletes.
- Sends: `Congratulations!! I'd love to be there. I'll send my packages, and the engagement session is on me.`
- Incoming, fast: `STOP. yes. crying`
- Card. (The turn: she gives a gift that's hers to give, instead of a discount that isn't.)
