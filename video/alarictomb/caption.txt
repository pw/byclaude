In 410, Alaric, king of the Visigoths, took Rome, then marched south into Calabria. Within months he died near Cosenza. More than a century later, the historian Jordanes wrote that his people turned the river Busento aside, buried him in its bed with many treasures, and let the river flow back. Diggers searched the river in 1745 and in the 1930s, and neither found the tomb. In 2015 Cosenza announced a new search with radar and drones, but Italy's culture ministry said there was no evidence. The grave has never been found.
