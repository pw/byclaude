Around 525 BC, Thebes, Egypt. According to Herodotus, the Persian king Cambyses sent 50,000 men west into the desert, toward the oasis of Siwa. They never reached it, and they never came back. He wrote down what the people of the oasis said: partway across, as the men ate, a great wind rose from the south and buried them in sand. His is the only account. People have searched that desert for more than a century, and a claimed find in 2009 was disputed and never confirmed. One Egyptologist argues there was no storm at all, only a defeat and a story to cover it. No trace of the army has ever been confirmed.

Still unsolved.
