In October 1969, thieves cut Caravaggio's Nativity from its frame above the altar of the Oratory of San Lorenzo in Palermo, Sicily. The canvas was nearly nine feet tall. The FBI named the theft one of its top ten art crimes. Over the years, investigators were told it was burned, hidden in a barn, or sold abroad. None of those leads brought it back. Today a copy hangs in its place. The original has never been found.

Still unsolved.
