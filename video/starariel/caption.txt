January 17, 1949, Bermuda. Less than a year after the Star Tiger vanished, British South American Airways sent the same kind of airliner, an Avro Tudor IV named Star Ariel, to Kingston, Jamaica. This time the weather was fine, and the captain reported good visibility. About an hour out, he sent a routine message: he was changing radio frequency. Nothing more was heard. Warships and aircraft searched for days and found no wreckage at all. The inquiry could not find a cause, and the wreck has never been found.

Still unsolved.
