September 22, 1979, the southern Indian Ocean. One American satellite, Vela 6911, recorded a double flash of light. Vela satellites had recorded 41 double flashes before, and every one had been a nuclear test. But this satellite was ten years old, past its design life, and its radio-pulse sensor had already failed. Its two light sensors both caught the flash, yet their readings disagreed on the second pulse. In 1980, a panel for the White House suggested a tiny meteoroid had struck the satellite, its debris catching the sun. Later analysis judged a meteoroid much less likely, and other evidence pointed to a test. The United States has never confirmed what its satellite saw.

Still unsolved.
