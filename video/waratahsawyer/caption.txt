July 1909, Durban, South Africa. A passenger named Claude Sawyer left the liner Waratah there, though he was booked on to Cape Town. She sailed on without him, and no one aboard was ever seen again. At the Board of Trade inquiry in London, he said the ship rolled heavily and was slow to come back upright. He said that one night on the voyage he dreamed three times of a man with a long sword. He also said he dreamed she was struck by a great wave and disappeared. The inquiry judged she probably capsized in a storm, but could not say how. The Waratah has never been found.

Still unsolved.
