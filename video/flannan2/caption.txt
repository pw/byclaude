December 1900, Eilean Mòr in the Outer Hebrides of Scotland. A passing steamer saw that the Flannan Isles lighthouse was dark. Storms held the relief boat back until Boxing Day, eleven days after the light was seen to be out, and when it landed no one came down to meet it. The lamps had been cleaned and refilled, and one keeper's oilskins were still inside. High on the west cliff, a box of ropes kept about 110 feet above the sea had been smashed open. The lighthouse board's inquiry blamed an unusually large wave. Not one of the three keepers was ever found.

Still unsolved.
