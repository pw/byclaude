Autumn 1931, off the Arctic coast of Alaska. Pack ice trapped the Baychimo, a Hudson's Bay Company cargo steamer with a hold full of furs. Most of her crew were flown out. The rest watched from shore as a blizzard on November 24 swallowed her. Hunters and prospectors kept finding her, drifting and empty. Some climbed aboard. No one ever brought her in. The last reported sighting was in 1969, frozen in the ice. She has never been found.

Still unsolved.
