July 27, 1909, off the coast of South Africa. One evening, the crew of the steamer Harlow saw a ship behind them, pouring smoke. After dark they could see her lights. Then came two great flashes, seconds apart, and the lights were gone. The Harlow's officers put it down to bush fires ashore, and her captain did not report it when she reached Durban the next day. Two months later her captain heard that the liner Waratah was missing. She had left Durban the evening before the flashes, with 211 people aboard. He became sure he had seen her. The Waratah's owners doubted it. The Waratah has never been found.

Still unsolved.
