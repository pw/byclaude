In 1484, a giant bell was cast for the Shwedagon Pagoda in what is now Yangon, Myanmar. It was said to be the largest bell ever made. In 1608, the Portuguese mercenary Filipe de Brito seized it, to melt it down for cannons. Where the Yangon and Bago rivers meet, the vessel carrying it sank under the weight. Divers have searched for decades, and in 2014 a team claimed to have found it, but the claim was never proven. It may lie beneath deep mud. The bell has never been found.

Still unsolved.
