November 1955, the South Pacific, north of Fiji. A small ship was found drifting with no one aboard. The Joyita had left Samoa five weeks earlier on a two-day run to Tokelau, with 25 people aboard. She was more than 600 miles off course, partly submerged, kept afloat by the cork lining her hull. Her radio was tuned to the distress frequency. The clocks had stopped at 10:25. The life rafts were gone. No one aboard was ever found.

Still unsolved.
