1939, London. The cryptographer Alexander d'Agapeyeff published a book called Codes and Ciphers. At the end, he printed a challenge: rows of numbers in groups of five. He later reportedly admitted he no longer knew how he had enciphered it. The challenge was left out of later editions. Codebreakers have studied it ever since, by hand and then by computer. No solution has ever been generally accepted.

Still unsolved.
