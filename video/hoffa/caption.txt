July 30, 1975, Bloomfield Township, Michigan. Jimmy Hoffa, former president of the Teamsters, went to the Machus Red Fox restaurant for a 2 p.m. meeting. Around 2:15, he called his wife from a pay phone and said no one had shown up. He told her he'd be home by 4 to grill steaks. He never came home. His car was found in the restaurant parking lot. His body has never been found, and no one has ever been charged. The FBI says the case is still open.

Still unsolved.
