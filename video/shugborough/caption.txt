Shugborough Hall, Staffordshire, England, the mid-1700s. A stone monument was raised in the gardens. Beneath a carved scene of shepherds at a tomb, eight letters: O U O S V A V V, with a D and an M below. The carving copies a painting by Nicolas Poussin, but in mirror image. In 2004, two Bletchley Park veterans took on the letters. No conclusive answer emerged. Theories include Latin initials, a love message, even later graffiti. No reading has ever been accepted.

Still unsolved.
