June 1899, London. Two years after Dora Penny received Edward Elgar's unreadable cipher, she became a piece of his music: Variation X, "Dorabella", in his Enigma Variations. In the programme for the first performance, Elgar said that another, larger theme goes through the whole set, but is not played. He told Dora he thought she, of all people, would guess it. She never did. Elgar died in 1934 without naming it. Dozens of answers have been proposed, and none has ever been generally accepted.

Still unsolved.
