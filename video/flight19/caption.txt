December 5, 1945, off the coast of Florida. Five Navy Avenger bombers took off from Fort Lauderdale on a training flight, with fourteen men aboard. The flight leader radioed that his compasses had failed. He believed he was over the Florida Keys. The radio calls faded into static, and the planes never came back. That night, a flying boat with thirteen men went out to search. It was never heard from again. The Navy's board recorded the loss as causes or reasons unknown. No wreckage of the five bombers has ever been found.
