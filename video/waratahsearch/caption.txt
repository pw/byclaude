July 1909, off the coast of South Africa. The liner Waratah left Durban for Cape Town with 211 people aboard, and none was ever seen again. In 1999, searchers announced they had found her: explorer Emlyn Brown's sonar had located a wreck whose outline seemed to match. Two years later, a dive showed it was another ship, the Nailsea Meadow, a wartime freighter lost in 1943. In 2004, after more than twenty years, Brown said he had exhausted his options and no longer knew where to look. The Waratah has never been found.

Still unsolved.
