1838, Hungary. A nobleman gave his library to the Hungarian Academy of Sciences, and in it was a small book no one can read. The Rohonc Codex runs to 448 pages, written in an unknown script of hundreds of different signs. Among the text are 87 drawings, with Christian, Muslim and pagan symbols side by side. Its paper was likely made in northern Italy in the 1500s; when the writing was added is unknown. Some scholars think it is a forgery. Several decipherments have been proposed, and none has been widely accepted.

Still unsolved.
