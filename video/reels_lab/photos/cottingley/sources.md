# Sources: cottingley

Image: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cottingley_Fairies_5.jpg (licence templates PD-old-auto-expired|1988 + Simultaneous US publication; en.wikipedia hosts the same photo as PD-US, "Cottingley-sunbath.jpg", first published Strand Magazine 1921). Original scan 1362x1616 downloaded unaltered.

- Two cousins, Cottingley, Yorkshire; Elsie Wright 16 and Frances Griffiths 9 in 1917; five photographs total: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cottingley_Fairies ; first two taken 1917 with a Midg camera: https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co469836
- Conan Doyle published them (Strand Magazine, Dec 1920 article, later 1921 Strand and 1922 book The Coming of the Fairies): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cottingley_Fairies ; timeline also at https://artsandculture.google.com/story/lgVB6Ceti9WVAw
- 1983 admission that the photographs were faked with cardboard cut-outs (hatpins): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cottingley_Fairies ; https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co469836 ("admitted that they had faked the images in an article in 1983"); https://garyreddin.substack.com/p/the-cottingley-fairies ("four of the five original photos" admitted fake)
- Last three photographs taken August 1920; fifth ("Fairies and Their Sun-Bath" / "The Fairy Bower") published Strand March 1921: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cottingley_Fairies ; https://artsandculture.google.com/story/lgVB6Ceti9WVAw
- Frances insisted the fifth was genuine: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cottingley_Fairies ; https://artsandculture.google.com/story/lgVB6Ceti9WVAw ("Frances insisted it showed real fairies")

Not stated in caption because sources conflict: Elsie's position on the fifth (Wikipedia/Google Arts: she called it faked/double exposure; one blog says both held it genuine).
