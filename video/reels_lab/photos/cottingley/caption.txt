In 1917 two cousins in Cottingley, Yorkshire, aged 16 and 9, photographed what they said were fairies, and Arthur Conan Doyle later published the pictures as evidence. In 1983 the women admitted the pictures were staged with paper cut-outs and hatpins, but they split on the last one. Elsie Wright called this fifth photo, taken in August 1920, a fake like the rest. Frances Griffiths insisted it was genuine.
