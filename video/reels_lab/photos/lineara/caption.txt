A clay tablet from the Minoan palace of Zakros on Crete, written in Linear A before the palace was destroyed around 1450 BC. Scholars can sound out many of its signs using values borrowed from Linear B, deciphered in 1952, but the words that result match no known language. About 1,400 Linear A inscriptions survive. The numbers can be read; the language cannot.
