On 30 June 1908, something exploded over Siberia and flattened about 2,150 square kilometres of forest. When Leonid Kulik's expedition reached the centre in 1927 there was no crater, only trees stripped bare and left standing, with the fallen ones laid out pointing away from the middle. The accepted explanation is an asteroid or comet fragment bursting in the air, and no piece of it has ever been formally identified.
