No one can read the line of signs on this steatite seal, made in the Indus Valley about 4,000 years ago and only 3.5 centimetres wide. Some 5,000 inscriptions have been unearthed since 1924, averaging about five signs, and no bilingual text exists to help. Tamil Nadu announced a $1 million prize for a reading in 2025; none has been accepted.
