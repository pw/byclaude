In 1931 the Hudson's Bay Company steamer Baychimo was left behind in the Arctic ice off Alaska, after her crew took off the most valuable furs. She did not sink then. Prospectors, explorers and Inuit reported her drifting for decades, and in 1969 she was seen frozen in pack ice. Nobody has found her since.

Still unsolved.
