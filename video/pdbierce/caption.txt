In October 1913, Ambrose Bierce, the 71-year-old writer they called Bitter Bierce, left Washington, D.C., on a last journey south toward revolutionary Mexico. A Civil War veteran and the author of The Devil's Dictionary, he reportedly meant to travel with Pancho Villa's army as an observer. His last known letter is dated Chihuahua, 26 December 1913, and says he was leaving for an unknown destination. After that, nothing certain. Some say he was at Ojinaga, on the border, in January 1914; others point to Sierra Mojada, or to Marfa, Texas; a few doubt he ever crossed at all. The date and place of his death have never been established. The images are period photographs, all public domain.
