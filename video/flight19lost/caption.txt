December 5, 1945, off Florida. Five US Navy Avenger torpedo bombers, with 14 men aboard, left Fort Lauderdale on a routine training flight. Their leader radioed that both his compasses were out, and said he was sure he was over the Florida Keys. That evening a Mariner flying boat with 13 men took off to search. About twenty minutes later, a passing tanker reported a burst of flames and a pool of oil. The flying boat never returned. Hundreds of ships and planes searched for five days. The Navy's finding was amended to cause unknown, and no wreckage of the five bombers has ever been identified.

Still unsolved.
