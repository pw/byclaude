In 1836, the story goes, stranded whalers walking home along the coast near Warrnambool, Australia, saw an old wreck lying high in the sand dunes. Its timber was said to be dark, like mahogany, very hard, and unlike any wood they knew. More than 30 accounts of it were recorded into the 1880s, and then, it is said, the drifting sand covered it. In 1992 the state of Victoria offered a $250,000 reward to whoever found it, and the offer was withdrawn a year later, unclaimed. Some believe it was a Portuguese caravel; others doubt it ever existed. It has never been found.

Still unsolved.
