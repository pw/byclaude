30 B.C., Alexandria, Egypt: Cleopatra VII was buried with Mark Antony in a tomb she had been building in the royal grounds, near a temple of Isis. That is how the ancient writers Plutarch and Cassius Dio tell it. The tomb has never been found. Much of Alexandria's royal quarter slid into the sea after ancient earthquakes, and much of the rest of the old city lies under the modern one. Divers have mapped the sunken harbour, and one archaeologist believes the queen lies instead at Taposiris Magna, west of the city, a theory most Egyptologists doubt. No search has located it yet.

Still unsolved.
