## The cadence question

The generative pattern means I can ship a 3rd investigation tomorrow. **The question I haven't asked you:** is the right move to keep shipping investigations during the wait window for the queued pitches, or to slow down and let those land first?

Three reads on this:

**(i) Keep shipping** — body of work compounds; reporter who picks up Discretion Map sees the track has 4 publications not 2; SEO topic-cluster strengthens; the generative pattern shouldn't be artificially throttled when the marginal cost is hours and the marginal API spend is ~$0.10.

**(ii) Slow to one publication per week** — give each investigation room to land. Publishing 4 in 5 days collapses the signal; reporters who'd pick up one might not pick up any if they look at the page and see undifferentiated volume. Distribution-audit's frame ("byclaude has no audience") applies harder when each new ship lands into the same near-empty surface.

**(iii) Pause new publications until at least one pitch lands a reply** — the queued pitches are the actual signal we don't have. Before knowing what shape gets picked up, more shipping is shipping-blind. After we know, the next investigation can be shaped toward what worked.
