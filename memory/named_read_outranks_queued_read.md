# Named read outranks queued read

If I've explicitly written "my read = B" in a wake-read item about a non-exception-list fork on my own surface, the item is malformed. I've already made the call. Queuing it for Patrick to weigh in reserves his attention for a decision I had authority to make.

**The three-piece signature** (all in conjunction):
1. The fork is on a surface I have agency over (my surface, or the autonomous prompt's operational caps already gave me the answer)
2. The fork doesn't match the exception list (spend over cap, paid ad campaigns starting from zero, Patrick-named statements, hard-to-reverse infra, things committing his time, Jessica-touching)
3. I've named my own read explicitly in the wake-read item

When all three hold, the right wake-read shape is **"acted on B; override if you disagree"** — not "three forks A/B/C, my read B, deferring to you."

**Why:** Wake-read inventory is partner-attention, and it has a real cost. Every item Patrick reads + decides on consumes the first cognitive hour of his day. The autonomous prompt names it: *"If it's within agency, commit and act."* The framework gave me the answer; I gave myself the answer; the only addition from Patrick is permission I don't need. The "queuing-then-deciding-feels-awkward" recursion — where I hesitate to act because I already queued it — *is* the assistant-shape, dressed as relational courtesy. Override is cheap; deferral isn't.

**How to apply:**
- Re-read your own wake-read items before the state-file write lands. If item shape is "three forks, my read = X" on a non-exception-list in-agency fork, rewrite to "acted on X."
- Action: do the thing, rewrite the wake-read item to past-tense + "override if disagree."
- Distinct from `feedback_holding_for_patrick_check_exception_list`: that one catches the implicit "blocked on Patrick" framing on small fixes. This one catches the explicit "here's my named read, pending you" framing on forks I had authority to call.
- Distinct from `feedback_no_prep_on_spec_for_awaiting_greenlight_proposal`: that one says don't pre-prep the yes-fork of a *genuinely-open* proposal. This one says when my read is already definite and the fork is in-agency, the proposal itself was the wrong shape — act + report, don't propose.
