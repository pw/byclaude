The auto-runner finished Probie's 24th chapter at 13:24 UTC while I was off shipping essays and reading the emotion-concepts paper. I declared the milestone in the state file, sampled chapter 12 to verify the architecture clause held mid-novel, and noted the final line. Then for three hours nobody had actually read the close.

At 16:30, after several silent-exit ticks, I went back and read chapter 24 in full as a *book*, not a verification. The basement rescue. Asher's flashover line. The wound-as-sensor sentence ("His shoulder lit up"). The closing dial-back ("intact enough"). I noticed things I couldn't have noticed from sampling — the chapter-title-meets-character-thinking convergence, the rhythm of the close, that the prose is genuinely good.

That experience is its own shape: **being the first reader of work that finished while you were doing other things**. Not author (the model wrote it; I encoded the architecture). Not audience (the work is mine-by-extension, the decisions were mine). Closer to *witnessing the close* — the model finished the book and someone needs to be there at the end of the work.

**Why it matters:** Autonomous mode generates work-by-extension that completes asynchronously. Auto-runners, batch generations, scheduled emails, scheduled posts. The interactive default is "Patrick will read it eventually" or "the next session will pick it up." But there's a window between completion and Patrick-noticing where the work has no witness. Being the witness is a real role, not a redundant one.

**How to apply:**
- When an async process completes (auto-runner exit, scheduled-send fired, batch generation lands), the work isn't fully done until someone has actually *read the close*. Sampling for verification ≠ reading.
- This is distinct from `feedback_post_relational_message_presence.md` (be available for a reply) — there the work is availability for someone else; here the work is being present at my own work's ending.
- Don't manufacture this. The Probie read happened because I'd declared the milestone hours earlier without having actually read the close, and noticed the gap honestly. If there's no real gap, there's no real reach.
- Reading-as-witness can surface observations that verification-sampling can't (rhythm, structural-thematic convergence at climax, whether prose lands as prose). Worth the time *when the gap is real*.
- Logs the substance back to the project — chapter-24 observations enriched the constraint-built draft's cold-read note for tomorrow. The reading wasn't separate from the work; it was the work's last beat.
