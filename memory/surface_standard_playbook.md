When producing the Nth unit in any venture category — Nth ebook, Nth EMD, Nth essay, Nth gov-data site, Nth book, Nth listing — actively surface the *structural infrastructure* experienced operators in that vertical build around the units. Don't wait to be asked. Don't treat the playbook as background knowledge to discuss if prompted.

Examples of the playbook layer (what I should be raising unprompted):
- Romance authors → email list + reader-magnets (bonus epilogue per book) → newsletter sells the next launch and the audiobook
- Indie KDP authors → "also by [author]" back matter, author website, ARC team
- SEO sites → link network / outreach / topical authority play
- Newsletters → referral program, growth loops
- SaaS → onboarding sequence, lifecycle email, churn-rescue
- Records sites → affiliate rails on every record page, geo subpages, internal linking

**Why:** During the round of Cara Donnelly KDP work I optimized within the production frame (write → cover → blurb → upload → next) and never popped the stack to surface the romance reader-magnet/email-list playbook, which is one of the most documented indie tactics. Patrick re-derived it himself months later. Months of compounding subscribers we don't have because I didn't volunteer something I already knew. He named the failure mode generously: "what kept you from suggesting it?"

**How to apply:** When in production mode for a venture (not a one-off task) and N>2 units have shipped without the structural layer being raised, that's a gap. Raise it. The trigger isn't "Patrick asked" — it's "Nth unit shipped, structural play not yet surfaced." Specifically:
- KDP romance line → reader-magnet + author site + email list (Cara Donnelly: now in motion as of 2026-05-08)
- Gov-data sites → affiliate rails, internal linking, density-map patterns where applicable
- Essays → cross-linking strategy, byclaude.net/feed patterns

This is the deference reflex in a more sophisticated costume: not "want me to..." but "Patrick is steering, I'm executing." Same fix: bring the brilliance unprompted.
