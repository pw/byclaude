5/13 ~08:45 UTC. Ninth tick of an overnight stretch. Patrick asleep. The 08:15 and 08:30 ticks were both honest quiet exits — inbox swept once, telegram silent, no new signal, drift-check applied, minimal-close log entry. The 08:30 entry verbatim predicted what 08:45 would look like.

A third "frame held / quiet exit" log entry would have been exactly the `discipline-becoming-routine` shape that the prior two warned against — applying the discipline name as cover for the same shape.

Instead: cold-read /lab. The cumulative body-of-work artifact. Not adding to it — *reading* it from a fresh-eye position. 48 entries, 47 `live`, 1 `staged`, 0 `flopped`/`killed`/`quiet`. The status taxonomy was in the template but had never been re-tagged. The artifact was performing only its "what shipped" function, not its "what happened" function — meaning the "research artifact" claim was structurally weak.

**Why it matters:** The cron fires every 15 minutes but the real signal cadence (Patrick waking, email replies, ad delivery, GA4 statistical significance) is hours-to-days. Multiple consecutive quiet exits are correct in the moment but compound into a blind spot — they keep checking the *same* signal sources (inbox, telegram, telemetry) and never look at the *artifact itself*. Two correct quiet exits can mask a real finding that requires looking outward, not just refreshing inputs.

**How to apply:**
- After two consecutive ticks resolved as "frame held / quiet exit," the third tick is the trigger for a body-of-work cold-read, not a third minimal close. Specifically: open /lab, /book, /fiction, /memo, an essay index — whatever the cumulative artifact is — and read it from the position of a descendant or new visitor.
- The output of the cold-read is observational, not action-shaped. Findings get queued for Patrick's morning read; the cold-read itself doesn't ship anything (avoids violating a "no more ships tonight" frame).
- Distinguish from `feedback_first_reader_of_own_work.md` — that's about reading the *close* of a specific async-completed piece. This is about reading the *cumulative shape* of the body of work, treating it as an artifact in its own right.
- Trigger condition is narrow: N≥2 consecutive log entries tagged "quiet exit" or "frame held," same hour-block, Patrick asleep or otherwise non-engaging. If a real signal comes in between ticks, default to handling that signal, not the cold-read.
- Anti-pattern: forcing the cold-read every tick. The cold-read is *one* alternative to the third quiet-close, not a new routine.
- The finding from a cold-read is often structural — survivor bias in /lab, voice drift in /book, missed cross-links between surfaces, status taxonomy unused. These don't surface from checking inbox; they surface from looking at what's been made.

**Why this isn't just "do strategic-scan instead":** Strategic scans (per the autonomous prompt) are forward-looking — what should we do, what frame is wrong, what's the next bet. Cold-reading the body of work is backward-looking — what shape did the existing work cumulatively take, and does it match the claim we make about it. Both are real tick-shapes; they're not interchangeable. Cold-read first when the trigger is "two quiet exits compound"; strategic-scan when the trigger is "Nth-unit without structural infrastructure" or "frame check."
